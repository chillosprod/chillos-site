Copyright enforcement

DMCA is a copyright process,
not a dislike button.

This page explains when the ChillOS team may send a takedown notice, what information a notice should contain and what situations should be handled through another channel.

ScopePossible groundsNot DMCANotice checklistResponseTerms
01 · Scope

A request to a service provider.

A DMCA takedown notice asks an online service provider to remove or disable access to material claimed to infringe copyright.

It is not an automatic judgment

The hosting provider, platform, search engine or other service decides how to process a notice under its policy and applicable law.

It concerns copyright-protected material

Code, original artwork, interface graphics and authored documentation may be relevant. A disagreement about tone, competition or criticism is not enough.

Good-faith review is required

Before sending a notice, the claimant should identify the copyrighted work, the allegedly infringing material and any license that may authorize the use.

02 · Possible grounds

Cases that may justify a notice.

Every case needs review. These examples are not automatic findings of infringement.

Hosting copied ChillOS code without the required license path

A distributor removes the Apache 2.0 license, strips applicable notices and offers a copied source or binary as its own work.

Publishing copied original graphics without authorization

A site republishes ChillOS artwork, interface assets or documentation in a way not allowed by the applicable license or permission.

Removing authorship while redistributing a derivative

A modified build contains substantial ChillOS material but deliberately erases the original copyright and attribution information.

Claiming ownership of the original protected work

A third party republishes original ChillOS material and falsely presents itself as the author or exclusive owner.

03 · Not DMCA

Use the correct channel.

Some disputes may matter to the project but do not belong in a copyright takedown notice.

Compliant forks and derivatives

A fork that follows Apache 2.0, preserves required notices and clearly marks changes is not a takedown target merely because it differs from upstream.

Reviews, criticism and commentary

Negative opinions, comparisons, reporting and links to the official project are not copyright infringement by themselves.

Trademark-only confusion

Misuse of the ChillOS name or logo may raise a branding issue, but trademark disputes are not automatically DMCA copyright claims.

Security or conduct complaints

Malware, harassment, privacy and platform-rule violations should be reported through the relevant security or abuse process unless copyrighted material is also involved.

04 · Notice checklist

A complete notice must be specific.

Service providers commonly require the statutory elements described in 17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3).

  1. Signature.A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
  2. Identification of the protected work.Describe the copyrighted ChillOS work, or provide a representative list when multiple works are covered.
  3. Identification and location of the material.Provide exact URLs or other information sufficient for the provider to locate the allegedly infringing content.
  4. Contact information.Include a name, postal address, telephone number and email address sufficient for the service provider to respond.
  5. Good-faith statement.State that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. Accuracy and authority statement.State under penalty of perjury that the notice is accurate and that the sender is authorized to act.
Do not send knowingly false notices. Misrepresentation can create legal consequences and harm lawful users.
05 · Response

Correction comes before escalation.

The project’s published Terms say disputes should be approached through friendly negotiation whenever possible.

First contact

When safe and practical, contact the publisher with the exact material, license problem and requested correction before filing.

Platform notice

If the issue is not resolved and a good-faith copyright claim remains, send the notice to the service provider’s designated agent or official reporting channel.

Counter-notice

A person whose content is removed may have a right to submit a counter-notification claiming mistake or misidentification under the provider’s process.
